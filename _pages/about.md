I am a PhD student in economics at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), supervised by Philipp Lergetporer. My research sits at the intersection of **behavioral economics**, **experimental methods**, and **environmental and climate behavior**.

My current work follows two main strands:

- **Intergenerational climate behavior**: how climate preferences are transmitted within families, including from children to their parents.
- **AI and labor-market beliefs**: how people form beliefs about the labor-market consequences of artificial intelligence.

Before the PhD, I completed the Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's Degree in Quantitative Economics, specializing in mathematical economics. My master's thesis, supervised by Philippe Bich, was on network formation theory.
