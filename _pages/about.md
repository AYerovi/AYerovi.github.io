I am a PhD student at the [Technical University of Munich (TUM)](https://www.tum.de/en/about-tum), supervised by [Philipp Lergetporer](https://sites.google.com/lergetporer.at/philipplergetporereconomics). I teach and do my research from the [TUM Campus Heilbronn](https://chn.tum.de/who-we-are/campus-portrait), starting in the end of 2025. My research sits at the intersection of **behavioral economics**, **experimental methods**, and **network theory**.

Before being in the PhD I completed a Joint Master's Degree in Quantitative Economics (by University of Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne, Universitat Autónoma de Barcelona, UC Louvain, Universitá Ca' Foscari di Venezia, and Warsaw School of Economics). I have a diploma in Economics and Finance from Escuela Politécnica Nacional.
