I am a PhD student at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), supervised by Philipp Lergetporer and based at the Campus Heilbronn. My research sits at the intersection of **behavioral economics**, **experimental methods**, and **applied economic theory**.

Before the PhD I completed a Joint Master's Degree in Quantitative Economics (University of Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne, Universitat Autónoma de Barcelona, UC Louvain, Universitá Ca' Foscari di Venezia, and Warsaw School of Economics). I have a diploma in Economics and Finance from Escuela Politécnica Nacional.
