## About

I am a PhD student in economics at the Technical University of Munich, supervised by [Philipp Lergetporer](#). My research sits at the intersection of **behavioral economics**, **experimental methods**, and **environmental and climate behavior**.

My current work follows two main strands:

- **Intergenerational climate behavior** — how climate preferences are transmitted within families, including from children to parents.
- **AI and labor-market beliefs** — how people form beliefs about the labor-market consequences of artificial intelligence.

Before the PhD, I completed the Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's Degree in **Quantitative Economics**, specializing in mathematical economics, with a thesis on network formation theory supervised by Philippe Bich.
