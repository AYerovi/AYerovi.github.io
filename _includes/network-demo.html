Players form and cut links in the connections model (Jackson & Wolinsky, 1996). Each player values every other player they can reach, discounted by distance, and pays a cost for each direct link: ui(g) = Σj≠i δd(i,j) − c·di(g). At every step one pair revises its link if it is profitable to do so, until the network is pairwise stable. Move the sliders to change the incentives; hover over a player to see their payoff, or drag them around.
